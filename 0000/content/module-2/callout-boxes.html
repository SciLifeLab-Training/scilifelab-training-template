Callout boxes

Callout boxes draw attention to information that participants should notice. Use them to highlight useful details, practical advice, important messages, cautions, or warnings.

Each example below shows the Quarto syntax and how the callout appears when rendered.


Note

Use a note to provide additional information that supports the main text without interrupting its flow.

Markdown syntax

::: {.callout-note}
This is additional information that may help participants understand the topic.
:::

Rendered result

Note

This is additional information that may help participants understand the topic.

Tip

Use a tip to share practical advice or a helpful suggestion.

Markdown syntax

::: {.callout-tip}
Save your work regularly so you do not lose your changes.
:::

Rendered result

Tip

Save your work regularly so you do not lose your changes.

Important

Use an important callout to emphasise information that participants should pay particular attention to.

Markdown syntax

::: {.callout-important}
Complete the required steps before moving on to the next activity.
:::

Rendered result

Important

Complete the required steps before moving on to the next activity.

Caution

Use a caution callout to draw attention to something that could cause a problem if overlooked.

Markdown syntax

::: {.callout-caution}
Changing this setting may affect how the page is displayed.
:::

Rendered result

Caution

Changing this setting may affect how the page is displayed.

Warning

Use a warning callout for information about a significant risk or a potentially harmful action.

Markdown syntax

::: {.callout-warning}
Do not share personal or confidential information in publicly accessible materials.
:::

Rendered result

Warning

Do not share personal or confidential information in publicly accessible materials.

Using callouts effectively

Callouts are optional. Use them when highlighting information improves the learning experience. Avoid adding too many to a page, as this can make it harder for readers to identify what is important. You can also include Markdown formatting, such as bold text, lists, or links, inside a callout.

Markdown syntax

::: {.callout-tip}
**Before submitting your work:**

- Check that all required sections are complete.
- Review your text for clarity.
- Save your changes.
:::

Rendered result

Tip

Before submitting your work:

  • Check that all required sections are complete.
  • Review your text for clarity.
  • Save your changes.
